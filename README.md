## Maadhav

Electronics and communication engineering by training. My work sits at the
intersection of machine learning and systems: model compression, inference
runtimes, and computer vision applied to real measurement data.

I am principally interested in problems where a result must be both fast and
correct, and where the two constraints are in tension.

---

### Current work

**[neka-rt](https://github.com/vhmaadhav/neka-rt)** — A private, local-first inference engine.

**[semicon-driftsense](https://github.com/vhmaadhav/semicon-driftsense)** — Localization of a
high-resolution reference patch within a low-resolution SEM search frame under unknown zoom,
unknown rotation, and possible absence. Submitted for the Applied Materials Drift-Sense
challenge, SEMICON India 2026.

**[quantization-lab](https://github.com/vhmaadhav/quantization-lab)** — Post-training
quantization worked through from first principles.
